The many sides of PCA, made interactive

The same eigendecomposition seen as a best linear approximation, a maximum-variance projection, and a distance-preserving map. Each panel below is a self-contained custom element — pure client-side, no server.

1 · Maximize the variance

The blue ellipse is the shape of the data; the gold arrows are its eigenvectors. Drag the plane to spin the cream direction u, or press sweep. The variance along u, written uᵀΣu, traces out the green curve below — and its single peak sits exactly on the long eigenvector. Best linear approximation and maximum variance are the same statement.

2 · The covariance is a transformation

Press morph. A round, isotropic cloud is pushed through Σ½ into the data ellipse. Watch the gold eigenvector lines: their direction never changes — points on them only slide in and out — while the cream test vector turns off its original span. That invariance is what makes them the principal axes.

Built on a small canvas scene engine with D3-style number planes and KaTeX, in a 3Blue1Brown register. The remaining figures from the post — scree plot, reconstruction error, decorrelation, distance preservation — slot into the same primitives.
